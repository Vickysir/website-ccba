中文简介

扎根科克，服务华人

爱尔兰科克市是爱尔兰第二大城市，也是爱尔兰南部地区的政治、经济和文化中心，同时也是中国上海市的姐妹城市，并与深圳、杭州、无锡签署了发展合作谅解备忘录。

爱尔兰科克华人商会成立于2018年3月，是经科克市政府批准，并在中华人民共和国驻爱尔兰大使馆报备的综合非营利性社会团体，也是科克市唯一的华人华侨组织团体。会员来自中国大陆、港澳台地区以及马来西亚、菲律宾等国家和地区。

English Introduction

Rooted in Cork, connected globally

Cork is Ireland’s second-largest city and a political, economic and cultural centre of the Southern Region. It is a sister city of Shanghai and has signed cooperation agreements with Shenzhen, Hangzhou and Wuxi.

Established in March 2018, the Ireland Cork Chinese Business Association is an integrated non-profit organisation approved by Cork City Council and reported to the Embassy of the People’s Republic of China in Ireland. Its members reflect the diverse Chinese and overseas Chinese community living, working and studying in Cork.
